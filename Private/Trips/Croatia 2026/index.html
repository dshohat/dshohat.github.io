Guide's Tips

Family notes (girls 19 · 17 · 15)

Beat the heat & the crowds

Late July/August is 30–35 °C. Do old towns & Plitvice early morning; save beaches, boats and shaded cafés for midday. Carry water, hats and reef-safe sun cream.

Book these in advance
  • Dubrovnik City Walls — timed tickets (or Dubrovnik Pass)
  • Plitvice Lakes — timed entry sells out in summer
  • Split↔Hvar catamaran (Krilo / Jadrolinija) — passenger-only, ~1 h
  • Diocletian cellars & the Sting concert in Pula
Teen-favourite add-ons
  • Dubrovnik: sunset sea-kayak around the walls & Lokrum; Srđ cable car
  • Omiš: zipline over the Cetina canyon & gentle rafting — a highlight
  • Split/Hvar: Blue Cave + Stiniva speedboat day; Bačvice beach evenings
  • Istria: Baredine Cave, Lim Fjord boat, Pula's Hawaii beach
  • Zagreb: Museum of Broken Relationships, Tkalčićeva street, shopping
Driving & borders

Since the Pelješac Bridge (2022) you no longer cross Bosnia (Neum) — no passport stop. Croatia is now in Schengen & uses the euro, so no border or currency changes on the whole route. Old towns are pedestrian: park outside and book hotels with parking. Expect a one-way drop-off fee (pick up Dubrovnik, return Zagreb).

My one route tweak

On 27 July I'd choose Zadar over Krka. Swimming was banned at Krka's main falls in 2021, and you already get waterfalls at Plitvice + Rastoke. Zadar's Sea Organ & sunset are a relaxed, teen-friendly finish before Plitvice.
